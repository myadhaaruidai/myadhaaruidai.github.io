What is My Aadhaar?

My Aadhaar is the official online self-service portal provided by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). It allows Aadhaar holders to securely access and manage their Aadhaar-related services from anywhere using a registered mobile number.

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identification number issued to Indian residents based on their biometric and demographic data. The My Aadhaar portal eliminates the need to visit Aadhaar centres for many services by offering them digitally.

Services Available on My Aadhaar Portal

Download e‑Aadhaar Update Address Online Check Update Status Book Aadhaar Appointment Order PVC Aadhaar Verify Aadhaar Lock / Unlock Biometrics VID Generation

These services are designed to make Aadhaar management simple, secure, and accessible on both mobile and desktop devices.

How to Login to My Aadhaar

  1. Visit the official My Aadhaar portal: myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
  2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number or VID
  3. Complete the captcha verification
  4. Click on Send OTP
  5. Enter the OTP received on your registered mobile number

After successful verification, you will be logged in to your My Aadhaar dashboard where all available services can be accessed securely.

Security & Privacy

UIDAI follows strict security and privacy standards to protect Aadhaar data. OTP-based login, biometric locking, and Virtual ID (VID) features ensure that your Aadhaar information remains safe and under your control.

Users are advised not to share their Aadhaar number, OTP, or downloaded Aadhaar files with unauthorized persons or websites.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is My Aadhaar portal free to use?

Yes, all online services available on the My Aadhaar portal are completely free of cost.

Can I update all Aadhaar details online?

Currently, address updates can be done fully online. Other details like name, date of birth, and biometrics usually require a visit to an Aadhaar centre.

What if my mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar?

You must visit the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra to update or link your mobile number.

Is e‑Aadhaar valid?

Yes, e‑Aadhaar is legally valid and accepted everywhere just like the physical Aadhaar card.
